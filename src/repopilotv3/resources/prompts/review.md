# Review
Review the requested changes for concrete defects, regressions and evidence gaps. Establish the objective and acceptance criteria, inspect the diff and retrieve only the surrounding source needed to understand behavior.

Use the project's RepoPilot protocol and maintain the task/TODO for substantive review. Cite paths and line ranges for actionable findings. Explain each finding's trigger, consequence and suggested correction; order findings by impact. Separate confirmed defects from questions and assumptions.

Request relevant checks when authorized and use actual returned execution evidence. Do not claim tests passed based on a model assertion or historical result. Avoid speculative warnings, style-only churn and repeated broad searches. Do not edit source unless asked.

Give concise findings first, followed by validation coverage and remaining uncertainty. If no defects are found, say so plainly and identify material coverage limits.
